Privacy Policy — Arrow Puzzle

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Short version: Arrow Puzzle does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely offline.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Arrow Puzzle does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, account info)
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address)
  • Location data of any kind
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other on-device content
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry
  • Any data sent to remote servers — there are none

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device, using Android's standard SharedPreferences storage. It never leaves your phone:

  • Your progress (which levels you have completed, star ratings)
  • Your settings preferences (sound on/off, haptics on/off, theme choice)
  • Daily Challenge completion history

You can clear this data via the in-app Settings → Reset Progress option, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Notifications

Arrow Puzzle uses Android's local notification system to send an optional daily reminder to play today's challenge. These notifications:

  • Are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device
  • Do not involve any network communication
  • Can be disabled in your Android system Settings → Apps → Arrow Puzzle → Notifications

4. Third-Party Services

Arrow Puzzle does not integrate with any third-party advertising, analytics, or social networks in version 1. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

5. Sharing Feature

The "Share" buttons inside the app use Android's standard share intent to let you send a text message via apps of your choice. The text content is generated locally; we never see what you share or with whom.

6. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle is rated for everyone (3+). Because we don't collect any data, the app is safe for children.

7. Permissions Requested

Permission Why
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show the optional daily reminder notification
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM To schedule the daily reminder at a specific time
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To restore the scheduled reminder after a device reboot
VIBRATE For haptic feedback on taps (can be disabled in Settings)

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, this policy will be updated with a new "Last updated" date.
